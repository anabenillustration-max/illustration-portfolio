Saltillo 447

A commemorative municipal poster commissioned to celebrate the 447th anniversary of Saltillo, Coahuila. The graphic design honors amateur photographer Alejandro Carmona, who scaled the heights of the cathedral dome to capture a historic aerial photograph at the dawn of regional commercial photography.

Commission City of Saltillo
