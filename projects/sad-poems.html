Poems for a man who doesn't speak Spanish

Poems for a man who doesn't speak Spanish is a self-published zine collection of poems and illustrations exploring love, memory, and grief. The visuals depict the artist's house and the rain-slicked streets of Guadalajara during the 2025 rainy season.
