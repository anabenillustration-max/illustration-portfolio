Cinema Odyssey

An editorial book cover and series compiling essays by writers about cinema. The illustrations translate the poetic visual language of the essays into graphic landscapes, referencing Thodōros Angelopoulos's Landscape in the Mist (1988) and Mike Mills's Beginners (2010).
