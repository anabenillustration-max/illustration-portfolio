La Lune et l'oiseau
L'étoile et l'oiseau

The moon appears in the middle of the night, at first a thin crescent, then revealing itself in full. A bird flies to give it a kiss, but the moon has already disappeared.

A bird sees a small star fall from the sky. Finding it on the ground, the bird decides to carry it back to its nest. Although the bird offers it worms, that is not what a star needs; once placed inside the nest, the star gently illuminates the home.
