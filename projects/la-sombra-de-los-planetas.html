La Sombra de los Planetas

Digital illustrations depicting figures in Mexican culture, such as El Osito Bimbo, Fidel Castro, and Emperatriz Carlota, placed in meme poses. These pieces were created to accompany the narrative in 'La Sombra de los Planetas', a book by Gabriel Rodríguez Liceaga published by Penguin Random House.
