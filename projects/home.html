HOME

HOME is a comic about dreams. It won First Place in the Short Story Competition at the International Festival of Comics and Games in Łódź, Poland. Created as an intimate narrative visual essay, the zine features detailed ink drawings colored digitally to evoke a warm, contemplative atmosphere.

Project Type Comic / Zine
Year 2021
Medium Ink | Digital Color
